# METHODOLOGY REPRODUCIBILITY AUDIT

## 1. Overview & Research Integrity Protocol

This document provides a comprehensive, step-by-step reproducibility audit for the methodology presented in **Section 3 (Methodology)** of the journal extension manuscript. The entire empirical pipeline is frozen, fully automated, and verifiable through deterministic scripts and unit test suites located in `journal_extension/`.

### Core Integrity Safeguards:
1. **Zero 2017 Test Data Contamination**: No model parameter estimation, hyperparameter tuning, feature selection, sliding window optimization ($W=100$), neighborhood tuning ($K^*=100$), or initial inventory calculation accessed the 2017 out-of-sample test partition.
2. **Strict Multi-Horizon Causal Isolation**: All dynamic features enforce a minimum lag of $\tau \ge 7$ days, ensuring that no predictive feature intersects with the forward forecast execution interval $[t+1, t+7]$.
3. **Eligible Residual Filtering**: Historical nonconformity scores at decision time $t$ require $r \le t - L - 1$, guaranteeing that no realized demand from ongoing replenishment intervals enters the conformal quantile calculation.
4. **Permanent Methodology Freeze**: The experimental results generated by EXP-011 through EXP-020 are permanently frozen in `journal_extension/results/final_audit/`.

## 2. Data Provenance & Chronological Partition Boundaries

The empirical benchmark is based on the standard Kaggle Store Item Demand Forecasting dataset, comprising 500 daily time series across 10 retail stores and 50 distinct items:
- **Total Observations**: 913,000 daily sales records (1,826 calendar days per series $\times$ 500 series).
- **Time Range**: 2013-01-01 through 2017-12-31.

## 4. 12-Point Inventory Logic & Temporal Leakage Audit

Prior to freezing experimental results, the discrete-event simulation engine was audited across 12 formal operational verification criteria:

| Check # | Audit Criterion | Formal Verification Condition | Verification Status |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Check 1** | Inventory Non-Negativity | $OH_{i,t}^{\text{close}} \ge 0, \forall i \in [1, N], \forall t \in [1, T]$ | **PASSED** (0 negative records) |
| **Check 2** | Position Conservation | $IP_{i,t} = OH_{i,t}^{\text{start}} + OO_{i,t}$ identically holds at every morning decision step | **PASSED** (100% identity) |
| **Check 3** | Lead-Time Arrival Exactness | Order $Q_{i,t} > 0$ arrives on day $t+L$ with zero time distortion | **PASSED** (Exact lag matching) |
| **Check 4** | Pipeline Isolation | Zero orders received before full lead time $L$ elapsed | **PASSED** (Zero premature arrivals) |
| **Check 5** | Shortage Accounting | $\text{Shortage}_{i,t} = \max(0, y_{i,t} - OH_{i,t}^{\text{start}})$ charged strictly on unmet sales | **PASSED** (Exact demand balance) |
| **Check 6** | Lost Sales Handling | Unmet sales permanently dropped without backordering queues | **PASSED** (Lost-sales discipline verified) |
| **Check 7** | Ordering Cost Trigger | $K_{\text{order}} = \$10$ charged strictly when $Q_{i,t} > 0$ | **PASSED** (Charge strictly aligned with $Q>0$) |
| **Check 8** | Holding Cost Assessment | $h = \$0.20$ charged strictly on closing stock $OH_{i,t}^{\text{close}}$ | **PASSED** (Evaluated at day close) |
| **Check 9** | Safety Stock Independence | $SS_{i,t,L}^{(\beta)}$ computed using only morning conformal bounds | **PASSED** (Zero intra-day lookahead) |
| **Check 10** | 2017 Realization Isolation | $y_{i,t}$ accessed exclusively at Step 6 of the daily timing loop | **PASSED** (Morning steps 1-5 isolated) |
| **Check 11** | Pre-2017 Initial Stock | $OH_{i,1}^{\text{start}} = L \cdot \mu_i^{\text{pre-2017}}$ computed from 2013-2016 data only | **PASSED** (Zero 2017 initialization data) |
| **Check 12** | Zero Look-Ahead in ROP | $ROP_{i,t,L}$ uses only lag $\ge 7$ features and eligible residuals $r \le t-L-1$ | **PASSED** (Full temporal causality verified) |

## 6. Random Seed Governance & Determinism

To guarantee exact bit-level numerical reproducibility:
1. All stochastic algorithms and subsampling routines specify `random_state = 42` (or `seed = 42`).
2. The 1,000 bootstrap resamples utilize a fixed random number generator initialized with seed 42: `np.random.RandomState(42)`.
3. Model training for XGBoost regressors enforces deterministic histogram binning and thread consistency (`n_jobs = -1`).
