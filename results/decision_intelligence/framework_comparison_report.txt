========================================================================
COMPARATIVE EVALUATION: RULE RECOMMENDATIONS VS. DECISION INTELLIGENCE
========================================================================

1. STRUCTURAL CAPABILITY COMPARISON:
------------------------------------
 Evaluation Dimension      | Rule Recommendation Engine     | Decision Intelligence (E-IDSS)
 -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Prioritization            | Binary thresholds (No Ranking) | 0-100 Decision Score Ranking  
 Explainability            | Static text blocks             | SHAP local feature justifications
 Risk Awareness            | Not integrated                 | Volatility & Uncertainty scoring
 Actionability             | Generic inventory alerts       | Categorized strategic opportunity
 Executive Quality         | No cross-tab/priorities        | Rankings, matrices & summary reports

2. STATISTICAL ADVANTAGES:
--------------------------
 * Multi-Criteria Decisions: The Rule Engine uses only growth rate to decide actions.
   E-IDSS integrates growth, moving average trends, seasonality coefficients, local SHAP values,
   and historical demand stability. This prevents ordering surges on noisy outliers.
 * Risk-Aware Safety Stocks: Rather than flat multiplier buffers, E-IDSS calculates prediction
   uncertainty (RMSE) dynamically, adjusting safety actions based on forecast confidence.
