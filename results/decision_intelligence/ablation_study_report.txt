========================================================================
SYSTEM ABLATION STUDY: EVALUATION OF DECOUPLED CAPABILITIES
========================================================================

1. COMPARATIVE CAPABILITY MATRIX:
---------------------------------
 System Stage Configuration                           | Explain | Biz Intel | Decision | Value    
 --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 A. Forecasting Only (Standard ML)                    | None    | Low       | Low      | Baseline 
 B. Forecasting + SHAP (XAI ML)                       | Global  | Medium    | Low      | Incremental
 C. Forecasting + SHAP + Recommendations (Rule DSS)   | Global  | Medium    | Medium   | Moderate 
 D. Complete E-IDSS (Decision Intelligence)           | Local   | High      | High     | Maximal  

2. ABLATION DISCUSSION:
-----------------------
 * Stage A (Forecasting Only) yields raw numbers but offers no trust or business logic,
   limiting operational value since operators cannot evaluate why values are predicted.
 * Stage B adds global explainability, highlighting model coefficients but offering no local,
   actionable advice on specific store-item nodes.
 * Stage C integrates rule-based support, which flags decisions but lacks prioritization.
   The system suffers from 'alarm fatigue' as multiple nodes are flagged simultaneously.
 * Stage D (Complete E-IDSS) represents the full framework. By synthesizing scores, priority ranks,
   and risk metrics, the system enables strategic capital allocation, targeting only high-confidence
   opportunities and isolating high-risk nodes.
