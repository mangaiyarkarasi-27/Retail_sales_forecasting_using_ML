RESEARCH CONTRIBUTION: EXPLAINABLE INTELLIGENT DECISION SUPPORT
========================================================================

1. METHODOLOGICAL NOVELTY:
--------------------------
 Traditional retail forecasting pipelines output scalar demand values (units) and stop.
 This research extends standard pipelines by implementing a joint Decision Scoring and Risk
 Assessment Framework to synthesize actionable business outcomes directly.

 * Decision Score Formulation:
   Decision_Score = 0.30*Growth_Norm + 0.25*Trend_Strength + 0.20*SHAP_Impact +
                    0.15*Seasonality + 0.10*Stability
   By utilizing local Shapley values as a criteria weight in Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis,
   the system ensures predictions heavily backed by robust features receive priority.

 * Risk Score Formulation:
   Risk_Score = 0.25*Vol + 0.25*Variance + 0.25*Instability + 0.25*Uncertainty
   By mapping model test-set residuals (uncertainty) and historical coefficient of variation
   to risk bounds, the system protects against supply chain volatility.

2. PEER-REVIEW JUSTIFICATION (E-IDSS):
--------------------------------------
 This pipeline qualifies as an Explainable Intelligent Decision Support System because:
 1. Intelligent: Utilizes Tree-based ensembles (XGBoost) for high-accuracy forecasts.
 2. Explainable: Leverages local SHAP value decompositions to justify each recommendation.
 3. Decision Support: Translates predictions to risk-rated priority queues and compiles
    executive summaries ready for supply chain integrations.
